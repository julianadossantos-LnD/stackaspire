About the program

From individual contributor to intentional leader

StackAspire is designed to close the gap between doing great work and leading others to do great work. It's a structured, 6-week journey that equips StackAdapt employees — at any level — with the leadership fundamentals that matter most.

Whether you're stepping into your first people manager role or deepening your leadership presence as a senior IC, this program meets you where you are and moves you forward with clarity.

Applied, not theoretical

Every lesson includes practical frameworks you can apply to your real work immediately.

StackAdapt-specific context

Built with StackAdapt culture, values, and operating model in mind — not generic leadership content.

Self-leadership as the foundation

Great leadership starts from within. You'll develop clarity on your own style and strengths first.

Learning design

How this program works

Each week combines structured learning with reflection exercises — designed to fit into your real work, not interrupt it.

Structured lessons

Each week contains 4–6 bite-sized lessons with a clear narrative arc, from concepts to practical application.
